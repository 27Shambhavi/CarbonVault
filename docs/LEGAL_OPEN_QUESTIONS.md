# CarbonVault — Legal, Tax & Regulatory Open Questions (Phase 7 / Phase 11)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This document lists legal, tax, and regulatory questions that require professional review by a licensed Chartered Accountant (CA) and legal counsel prior to commercial live transactions. Antigravity and CarbonVault developers do not provide legal or tax advice.

---

## 1. Goods & Services Tax (GST) — India

| # | Question / Issue | Context & Current Assumption | Professional Review Needed |
|---|------------------|------------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1.1 | **Classification of Carbon Credits** | Are voluntary carbon credits (VCCs / VERs) treated as "goods" (movable property) or "services" under the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017? | Confirm applicable HSN code (e.g. HSN 9983 for environmental services or HSN 4907 for title documents) and tax rate (18% vs exempt). |
| 1.2 | **Marketplace Platform Role & Facilitation Fee** | CarbonVault connects buyers and sellers. Is CarbonVault liable for GST on the full credit transaction value or only on the platform convenience fee (e.g. 2-5%)? | Confirm whether CarbonVault acts as a "pure agent" under Rule 33 of CGST Rules, or as an e-commerce operator under Section 9(5). |
| 1.3 | **Cross-Border Corporate Buyers** | Corporate buyers located outside India purchasing credits generated by Indian afforestation projects. | Does this qualify as "Export of Services" under Section 2(6) of IGST Act (zero-rated supply under LUT without payment of IGST)? |
| 1.4 | **Invoicing Requirements** | Sequential invoice numbering, B2B e-invoicing thresholds (Turnover > ₹5 Cr), and mandatory buyer GSTIN capture on tax receipts. | Review automated sequential invoice format generated by CarbonVault. |

## 2. Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) — Direct Tax / Income Tax Act, 1961

|---|------------------|------------------------------|----------------------------|

| 2.2 | **Section 194Q (Purchase of Goods)** | Corporate buyers purchasing goods exceeding ₹50 Lakhs in aggregate from a seller in a financial year must deduct 0.1% TDS. | Confirm whether carbon credit purchases qualify under Section 194Q or Section 194O prevails. |
| 2.3 | **Equalisation Levy & Non-Resident Withholding** | Payments from foreign corporations to Indian platforms or vice versa. | Verify applicability of 2% Equalisation Levy or Section 195 withholding on foreign entity fund transfers. |
